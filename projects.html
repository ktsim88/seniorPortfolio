My Zeroni Emotional Supports

“Zeronis” are the animal mascots of the members of my favorite group, Zerobaseone. These Zeronis are part of the marketing through Line Friends and they make plushies of these mascots. I started making them in March 2025 and finished them in February 2026. I started them when I was learning to sew other mini stuffed animals. I realized that one of the felt colors looked like the ones from my favorite Zeroni. I make these animals in my free time and I enjoy the process of making them.
